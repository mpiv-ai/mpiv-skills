# Interview guide: {Participant}, {Firm}

{Day} {YYYY-MM-DD}, {start}-{end} {TZ}. {Meeting link}
Their business, from their site: {one sentence}.

**Goal: leave with ONE recurring workflow described well enough to write a one-page brief.** Everything else is secondary. If they offer two workflows, pick the one they do most often and park the other by name.

## Timeline

**1. Open (2 min).**
Thank them for the reply. Restate the frame in one breath: this is research, you will send a one-page brief on whatever workflow they pick, an aggregated summary later, and nothing identifies them or their clients without their explicit OK. Ask: "Alright to take notes?"

**2. Pick the workflow (4 min).**
- "Walk me through what happens between {the trigger their site describes} and {the start of the work}. What are the steps on your side?"
- "Of everything you do repeatedly across engagements, which chore do you dread most?"
- Likely candidates if they stall: {three or four from their site}.
- Commit: "Let's spend the rest of the time on that one."

**3. Deep dive on one real instance (8 min).**
Anchor to a specific recent occurrence, not the general case: "Take the last time you did this. Start to finish, what happened?"
- Trigger: what starts it, who initiates.
- Steps and tools: what they open, where things get typed, what gets retyped in a second place.
- Handoffs and waits: who they wait on, what stalls it.
- The ugly part: "Where does this go wrong when you're busy?" and "What do you double-check because you've been burned?"

**4. Cost and workarounds (4 min).**
- "How often does this come up, and roughly how long does one pass take?"
- "What have you tried? Templates, a VA, any software?"
- "Why doesn't the software you have fit this?"
- "If this ran itself and only asked you for judgment calls, what would it need to get right for you to trust it?"

**5. Wrap (2 min).**
- Confirm the deliverable: "You'll get a one-page brief on this from me, and a copy of the aggregated summary when the research wraps."
- Consent check: default is anonymous. Only if a quote or detail would name them: ask explicitly, otherwise skip.
- Referral: "Who else runs a practice like yours and thinks about this stuff?"

## Capture checklist for the brief

Workflow name and trigger. Cadence and hours per instance. Actors and handoffs. Tools touched, in order. Top two failure points. What they have tried. Their words for what "fixed" looks like.

If the workflow is briefable, add a row to the product-candidate register in the same pass as the brief.

## Guardrails

Do not pitch, propose solutions, or name what you are building. Your background comes up only if they ask. If they ask what you are building, the answer: still in research, the aggregated summary will show the pattern.

The guide may name the wrong workflow. The site describes the offer they sell. The call shows the chore they run. When the call goes somewhere else, follow the call.
