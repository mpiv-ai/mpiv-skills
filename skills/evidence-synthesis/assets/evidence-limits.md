# Evidence limits: sentence patterns

Write the section from the inventory's limit column. One plain sentence per limit. Patterns from the first run:

- Two counts, two populations: "The registry listed {N} entries. Automated discovery observed {M}. The sources count different populations. The figures show an incomplete record. They do not show duplicate licences."
- A sample: "The {workspace} review was a sample, not a full export."
- A source that never arrived: "The requested {export} was not received. {Seat use and licence use} therefore remain open."
- Out of scope by contract: "{Cost by tool} was outside the signed scope."
- Public practice as a guide: "Published practice can guide operating rules. It does not prove that a vendor fits {the client}."
- What the figures do not show: "These figures do not show active seats, duplicate licences, contract value, or savings."
- Raw material excluded: "Raw transcripts are excluded. The appendix holds client-safe summaries."
