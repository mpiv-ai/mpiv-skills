## The email that got the reply

The first sentence came from her site's services page. Everything after it is the fixed template.

```
Hi [First name],

I saw that [Firm] offers planned product-leadership coverage for parental leave, including a clean handoff when the product manager returns.

I've spent 32 years helping clients turn messy business needs into workable systems. I'm now researching how independent consultants handle recurring client workflows that existing software doesn't support well.

Would you be willing to discuss one of those workflows for 20 minutes?

This is research, not a sales call.

Afterward, I'll send you a concise, one-page opportunity brief based on the workflow we discuss. I'll also publish an aggregated summary and send participants a copy. I won't identify you, your clients, or quote you without explicit permission.

If you're open to it, just reply yes and I'll send the scheduling link.

Read about the research and who I am: https://mpiv.ai/research/client-workflows

Michael Isaac
Founder, MPIsaac Ventures
michael@mpiv.ai  ·  mpiv.ai

MPIV · [postal address]
If this isn't relevant, reply "no" and I won't follow up.
```

## Observation sentences from the run

Five of the twenty, with firm names removed. Each names one thing from the person's own site.

- "I saw that your firm offers planned product-leadership coverage for parental leave, including a clean handoff when the product manager returns."
- "I saw that your 20-minute Leak Check produces a profitability scorecard, tax strategy, and enterprise-value gap report."
- "I noticed your fractional operations work often begins with an operational health check before process mapping, SOP design, and deeper implementation."
- "Your onboarding moves from consultation into a Financial Assessment of job costing, WIP, and cash flow before month-to-month CFO support."
- "Your SOC 2 and FDA Software Readiness Assessment is a clear example of a repeatable diagnostic that turns regulated context into a concrete plan."

## What the call produced

One recurring workflow described well enough for a one-page brief. Four research themes. Two referrals: a person and an open-source tool. An agreed quarterly check-in. The participant also asked how the email had found her. This skill is the answer to that question.
