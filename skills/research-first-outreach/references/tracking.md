# Tracking

Log each send right after it goes. Michael used Twenty CRM: one person record, one company record, one note per touch. A spreadsheet works too. Write down every touch before the next one.

## Tracker columns

`first_name | last_name | email | company | site | segment | cohort | variant | status | first_sent_at | replied_at | booked_at | call_at | brief_sent_at | notes`

Status values, in order: identified, drafted, sent, replied, booked, closed.

## The note logged per send

This is the note attached to each person in the CRM after cohort 1 and cohort 2 went out, with the title punctuation simplified. Copy the shape.

```
Campaign: AI Workflow Research - Cohort 1
Occurred at: 2026-08-31T19:57:00.000Z
Direction: Outbound
Channel: Email from michael@mpiv.ai
Subject: Question about your client workflow
Variant: Direct booking
Status: Sent
Offer: 20-minute research interview plus a one-page Expertise-to-Software Opportunity Brief
Research page: https://mpiv.ai/research/client-workflows
Next action: Review for reply or booking; send a value-adding follow-up if there is no response.
```

## After the call

Add a second note: the date, the workflow discussed, every promise you made on the call as a numbered list, and the date each one was delivered. Check this list before the next quarterly touch.

## Cohort discipline

- 10 per day. Cohorts let you compare variants, and 10 a day from one mailbox does not look like bulk mail.
- Mix segments inside a cohort. Then a reply tells you about the message rather than the industry.
- Read the replies before sending the next cohort. Cohort 2 changed two lines after cohort 1.
